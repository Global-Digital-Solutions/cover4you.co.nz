How It Works

Simple. Fast. No Runaround.

From finding the right site to hearing back from a real local expert — Cover4You makes getting the right insurance straightforward for every Kiwi.

01

Find Your Specialist Site

Each Cover4You site is dedicated to one insurance niche — so whether you need cover for your boat, your business, or your cat, you land on a page built exactly for you.

02

Compare & Get Informed

Plain-English guides, policy comparisons, and FAQs help you understand exactly what you're buying — so you can make a confident decision without the jargon.

03

Tell Us What You Need

A short, simple form captures your requirements in minutes. No lengthy sign-ups, no unsolicited calls — just the details needed to get you an accurate quote.

04

A Local Expert Gets in Touch

Your enquiry goes directly to a licensed NZ insurance advisor or broker who will compare options, answer your questions, and find the best deal for your situation.

🤝 Broker Lead-Gen Model

The majority of our sites operate as broker referral sites. Visitors read, compare, and then submit a contact form. Qualified leads are forwarded to our licensed NZ insurance advisor network for follow-up, quoting, and placement.

Sites: Directors, Tradie, Buildings, Rental, Yacht, and more
⭐ Affiliate Comparison Model

Select sites operate as direct affiliate comparison sites where visitors compare policies side-by-side and click through to insurer quote pages. Insurers benefit from pre-qualified, intent-driven traffic directly to their funnel.

Sites: Cruise, Health, Cat Insurance
